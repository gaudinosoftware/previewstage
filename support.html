Chapter 1

Getting started

When you open PreviewStage you see Home: your recent projects and every template. From here you can start a new project, open one you've made, or open the Device Library with the round button in the bottom-right corner.

Create a project

  1. Click New Project (⌘N), or click any template to start with it.
  2. Choose a Platform: App Store for listing screenshots, or Social Media for posts and stories. Website and Press Kit are coming soon.
  3. Choose your Devices: iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple Watch, Vision Pro or Apple TV. For social media you can also choose No Device.
  4. Pick a Size. The recommended App Store size is marked; other sizes are adapted for you when you export. Use the Portrait / Landscape switch where it applies.
  5. Under Start, choose Start Blank or Choose a Style to pick a template.
  6. Name your project and click Create.

Manage projects

Right-click a project on Home to Open, Rename, Duplicate or Move to Trash. Projects save automatically as you work.

The editor at a glance

Every project opens in its own window, with four areas.

A simple ruleSelect something on the canvas and the Inspector shows its settings. Click an empty spot and the Inspector shows the current screen, the project, and tools that change every screen at once.

Chapter 4

Left sidebar

The sidebar has four tabs at the top. Hover over a tab to see its name.

Screens

Your screenshot sequence, in the order it will appear on the App Store, and the layers of the current screen.

Screens list
Each screen with a thumbnail and its headline. Click to show it; double-click to rename. Drag to reorder. Drop screenshots from Finder onto a screen to fill it and the screens after it.
+ (Add Screen)
Adds a new screen that follows your template.
All Screens
Shows every screen side by side, so you can design them together.
Layers
Everything on the current screen, top layer first, with a short note on what each one is (for example Mock frame · Screenshot or Headline). Click to select; hover to show the lock and eye buttons. Drag to change the order.
Across All Screens
Layers that stretch across several screens, such as a panorama background or a spanning device.

Right-click a screen to rename, duplicate or delete it, or to apply its layout to every screen.

Assets

Every image in the project. Drop screenshots anywhere in the window, or click Import Screenshots… (⇧⌘I). Replace All… swaps every screenshot in one step.

Right-click an image to Use in Selected Device, Insert as Image, Use as Screen Background, Set as Brand Logo, or remove it from the project.

Templates

23 art-directed styles, each previewed with your own headlines and screenshots: Centered Device, Side by Side, Oversized Type, Edge Crop, Floating, Stacked Devices, Split, Cinematic Dark, Minimal White, Editorial, Full-Bleed Screenshot, Typography First, Continuous Panorama, Spotlight, Hero Zoom, Rise, Color Block, Poster, Award, Big Number, Diagonal, Aurora and Noir.

Click a template to apply it to every screen. Right-click to apply only its layout to the current screen. Your text and screenshots stay in place.

Brand

The look of the whole project, set once. Linked text and devices on every screen follow it.

Brand card
Shows your brand's name. The ⋯ button applies a saved brand, saves the current one for other projects, or deletes saved ones.
Headline
Font, weight, color, tracking (letter spacing), leading (line spacing), case (As Typed or All Caps) and size.
Supporting Text
Font, weight, color, leading and size for the smaller line under the headline.
Layout
Text alignment, the margin around each screen, and Tone (Light or Dark).
Background
Solid, Linear, Radial or Image, with presets. Continue across all screens makes one background flow behind every screen, like a panorama.
Devices
The finish (color) used by mock devices that follow the Brand.
Apple Device Frames
How many official bezel packs you have, and a shortcut to the Device Library.
Logo
Choose your app icon or logo, then place it on a screen.
Apply Everywhere
Link All Text to Brand makes every headline and supporting text follow these settings.
Chapter 5

Canvas

Your screens, at their real proportions. Click to select, drag to move, and drag the handles to resize or rotate. Double-click text to edit it.

Moving around
Scroll with two fingers to pan; pinch or ⌘-scroll to zoom. Double-tap with two fingers to switch between fitting everything and 100%.
Smart guides
While you drag, a dashed red line marks the center of the screen and turns solid green when your layer is exactly centered. Layers snap to the center, edges and other layers. Turn guides on or off with the ruler button or ⌘;.
Canvas controls
At the bottom: zoom out, zoom level, zoom in, Zoom to Fit, Guides, and Panorama View (screens joined edge to edge, for designs that flow across them).
Adding screenshots
Drop images straight onto a device. An empty device shows Drop a screenshot with a Choose… button.
Apple's guidelines card
If a device with an official Apple bezel is rotated, tilted, cropped, shadowed or covered, a card appears in the top-right corner explaining what Apple asks for. Click Straighten to fix it, or Use Mock to switch to a mock frame. Hidden devices don't count.
Orientation changes
If you switch the project between portrait and landscape, your layout is kept and devices turn with it. A bar offers to re-apply your template, laid out fresh for the new shape.
Chapter 6

Inspector

The panel on the right always shows settings for what you're working on. Long sections can be collapsed with the chevron next to their title.

When nothing is selected: screen & project

Screen card
Which screen you're on (for example Screen 2 of 3), with arrows to move to the previous or next screen.
Screen Settings
The screen's name and background: Brand follows the Brand tab, Custom gives this screen its own fill. Duplicate and Delete act on the whole screen.
Project
Project name, platform, devices, size and orientation.
All Screens
Changes every screen at once: Frame (Official or Mock), Device, Pose, Shadow and Text Align. Each shows its current choice, or Mixed when screens differ. Below are Apply Layout to All Screens, Replace Screenshots… and Edit Brand Typography….

When a layer is selected

A path at the top, like ‹ Screen 1 / Device Layer, shows where the layer lives. Click ‹ Screen 1 to go back to the screen's settings. Next to the layer's name are buttons to lock and hide it.

Arrange
Position (X, Y), size (W, H) and rotation, with buttons to rotate 90° left or right. The align bar lines the layer up with the screen's edges or center. Order moves it forward or backward. Span Screens lets it stretch across neighboring screens (needs two or more screens).
Text
Font, weight, color, tracking, alignment and caps. Edit the words themselves by double-clicking the text on the canvas. Headlines and supporting text can be Linked to Brand or Detached to style them on their own.
Device
Frame (Official or Mock), Device, Color, and for iPhone Duo, Fold (Open or Closed). Then the Screen it shows, with Replace… and Remove, Fit (Fill or Fit) and Orientation (Portrait or Landscape).
Presentation
Quick looks: Flat, Floating and Tilted, plus Reset. Below, fine-tune the Pose (Flat, Turn Left, Turn Right, Lay Back) and its Amount.
Image & Crop
The image source, Fit and Mask, then crop with Scale and Offset. Reset Crop starts over.
Shape
Shape type, fill (Solid, Linear, Radial or Image) and stroke.
Symbol
Click the symbol to open the browser: search by name or meaning (try “thumbs” or “award”), or browse categories such as Popular, Communication, Commerce, Nature and Arrows. Twelve Quick picks sit underneath, plus Color and Weight.
Appearance
Opacity, blur, corner radius and shadow (color, radius and offset).

When several layers are selected

Hold ⇧ or ⌘ and click to select more than one layer. The Inspector then offers alignment, distribution (horizontally or vertically), Group and Ungroup, plus opacity and shadow for all of them.

Chapter 7

Device frames

Every device can use one of two kinds of frame. Switch with Frame in the Inspector, for one device or for all screens at once.

Mock

PreviewStage's own drawn frames. Always available, in several finishes, and free to tilt, rotate or shadow however you like.

Official

Apple's own product bezels, downloaded from Apple when you choose them. They match the real devices exactly, and Apple asks that they're used as-is.

Only downloaded Apple devices can be chosen. Ones you haven't downloaded are dimmed, with a ↓ button to download them right there. If you switch to Official before downloading anything, PreviewStage keeps your mock and offers the download. Your device switches over when it's ready.

Mock and Official each remember their own choice, so switching back and forth never loses your setup.

Chapter 8

Device Library

Open it from the round button on Home, from Download More… in the Device menu, or with ⌥⌘L.

  • Filter by All, iPhone, iPad, Mac, Watch or TV, and turn on Installed to see only what you've downloaded.
  • Click Get to download a pack. Installed packs show a green check.
  • The first time, you'll be asked to accept Apple's license for its marketing artwork. The bezels are Apple's, and they're always free in PreviewStage.
Chapter 9

Preview Mode

Click ▶ in the toolbar (⌥⌘P) to see your screenshots the way customers will. Choose a view at the top:

Product Page
Your screenshots in a row under your app's name, as on your App Store page.
Search Result
The first screenshots as they appear in App Store search.
Focus
One screenshot at a time, full size. Use the arrow buttons, ← → or Space.

Screens with an empty device are left out, and a note tells you which ones. Press Esc or close the window to return to the editor.

Chapter 10

Exporting

Click Export (⌘E).

  1. Choose which screens to export: All, Current, or Choose to pick them.
  2. Under Sizes, tick the sizes you need. Sizes Apple requires are marked Required. Other sizes are adapted automatically from your layout.
  3. Set how File Names are written; an example shows the result.
  4. Export. Images are saved as PNG without transparency, which App Store Connect requires.
Before you exportEvery device needs a screenshot: blank screens can't ship, so export waits until they're filled. If an Apple bezel doesn't follow Apple's guidelines, you'll see a warning first.

Chapter 12

Help & FAQ

An Apple device in the menu is dimmed. Why can't I choose it?

It hasn't been downloaded yet. Click its ↓ button, or open the Device Library and click Get. When the download finishes, you can choose it.

Why won't my project export?

At least one device has no screenshot. Blank screens can't go on the App Store, so add a screenshot to every device, or delete the empty screen. The export sheet shows how many are missing.

What does “Apple's guidelines” mean?

Apple asks that its product bezels are shown upright, uncropped, without added shadows or reflections, and with nothing on top. If you want a tilted or shadowed look, switch that device to a Mock frame, which you can style freely.

Why are some screens missing in Preview Mode?

Screens with an empty device are left out of the preview. Add a screenshot and they'll appear.

Can I use any SF Symbol?

Thousands of them. Apple's logo and symbols that represent Apple's own products and features aren't available, because Apple's license doesn't allow them in your artwork.

I changed the orientation and my layout looks different.

Your layout is kept and resized for the new shape, and devices turn with it. For a fresh layout, click Re-apply in the bar at the bottom of the canvas, or press ⌘Z to undo the change.

Where are my projects saved?

On your Mac, automatically, as you work. Open them any time from Home (⇧⌘0).
