Chapter 1

Getting started

When you open PreviewStage you see Home: your recent projects and every template. From here you can start a new project, open one you've made, or open the Device Library with the round button in the bottom-right corner.

Create a project

  1. Click New Project (⌘N), or click any template to start with it.
  2. Choose a Platform: App Store for listing screenshots, or Social Media for posts, stories and videos.
  3. For social media, choose the Network: Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Facebook, X, LinkedIn, Threads or Pinterest. Its sizes and video lengths are used throughout the project, and you can change it later in the Inspector.
  4. Choose your Devices: iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple Watch, Vision Pro or Apple TV. For social media you can also choose No Device.
  5. Pick a Size (App Store) or Format (social media). The recommended one is marked; for the App Store, other sizes are adapted for you when you export. Use the Portrait / Landscape switch where it applies.
  6. Under Start, choose Start Blank or Choose a Style to pick a template.
  7. Optionally, link your app if it's already on the App Store: paste its link or search for it, and the project starts with its name, icon and details, with its live screenshots in Assets. See Linking your App Store app.
  8. Name your project and click Create.

Manage projects

Right-click a project on Home to Open, Rename, Duplicate or Move to Trash. Projects save automatically as you work.

Chapter 2

The editor at a glance

Every project opens in its own window. Like a theater, it has three parts: Backstage, where everything is prepared; the Stage, where your screenshots are presented; and the Inspector, which checks every detail before the show.

Left

Backstage

Prepare your show: scenes, trailers, images, templates and your brand.

Center

Stage

Your screenshots, presented and designed at their real proportions.

Right

Inspector

Inspects every layer, scene and setting so it's just right.

A simple ruleSelect something on the Stage and the Inspector shows its settings. Click an empty spot and the Inspector shows the current scene, the project, and tools that change every scene at once.

Chapter 4

Backstage

Backstage is the panel on the left. It has five tabs at the top: Scenes, Assets, Trailers, Templates and Brand. The open tab's name shows above its contents; hover over the others to see their names.

Scenes

Your screenshot sequence, in the order it will appear on the App Store, and the layers of the current scene.

Scenes list
Each scene with a thumbnail and its headline. Click to show it; double-click to rename. Drag to reorder. Drop screenshots from Finder onto a scene to fill it and the scenes after it.
+ (Add Scene)
Adds a new scene that follows your template.
All Scenes
Shows every scene side by side, so you can design them together.
Creative Assets
For iPhone and iPad projects: the Header and Search Results images for iOS 27 App Store pages, or one Universal image for both. Click one to open it on the Stage. See Header & Search Results.
Layers
Everything on the current scene, top layer first, with a short note on what each one is (for example Mock frame · Screenshot or Headline). Click to select; hover to show the lock and eye buttons; drag to change the order. A broken-link icon marks an item detached from the Brand.
Across All Scenes
Layers that stretch across several scenes, such as a panorama background or a spanning device.

Right-click a scene to rename, duplicate or delete it, or to apply its layout to every scene.

Trailers

Your App Store videos (Apple calls them App Previews), or videos for your social network.

Trailers list
Each trailer with its poster frame, length and number of takes. Click one to open it on the Stage; right-click to delete it. App Store projects can have three, as Apple allows.
+ (New Trailer)
Starts a new, empty trailer.
Record
Every Simulator that's running shows here with a Record button. If none is running, it tells you how to start one. See Recording takes.
Import Takes…
Adds screen recordings you already have.
Apple's Rules / Tips
For the App Store, Apple's rules for App Previews. For social media, tips for your network.

Assets

Every image in the project. Drop screenshots anywhere in the window, click + next to the title, or click Import Screenshots… (⇧⌘I). Replace All… swaps every screenshot in one step.

Right-click an image to Preview it in its own window, shaped like the picture and sized to fit your screen, to Compare it with your scene, or to Use in Selected Device, Insert as Image, Use as Scene Background, Set as Brand Logo, or remove it from the project.

Compare puts the picture on the Stage next to the current scene, at the same height, and it stays as you move between scenes. It's also where you work on a screenshot: hover over any text in it and right-click: Add to Scene 1 adds it with the same words, size, weight and color, or, when the scene already has text, Transfer to Scene 1 lets you pick the text layer to put the words in (it keeps its style), with Add as New Text at the bottom. Copy Text copies just the words; click the clock to Clean Status Bar (9:41, full signal and battery; on Apple Watch, just the time); or click the eraser and drag over anything on a plain background to erase it. Everything can be undone with ⌘Z. Click × to stop comparing. Erase… and Clean Status Bar are also in the image's right-click menu, and on a device's right-click menu for its screenshot.

Preview just shows the picture in its own window, shaped like it and sized to fit your screen.

Templates

35 art-directed styles, each previewed with your own headlines and screenshots: Centered Device, Side by Side, Oversized Type, Edge Crop, Floating, Stacked Devices, Split, Cinematic Dark, Minimal White, Editorial, Full-Bleed Screenshot, Typography First, Continuous Panorama, Spotlight, Hero Zoom, Rise, Color Block, Poster, Award, Big Number, Diagonal, Aurora and Noir, plus Ledger, Glass Callout, Breakout, Magazine, Stickers, Night Glow, Two-Up, Photo Stack, Kinetic Type, Macro, Horizon Set (one sky across the whole set) and Bento.

Click a template to apply it to every scene. Right-click to apply only its layout to the current scene. Your text and screenshots stay in place.

While a Header or Search Results image is open, this tab shows templates made for it instead.

Brand

The look of your whole project, set once. Every scene that follows the Brand updates as you change it.

Brand card
Your brand's name. The ⋯ button applies a saved brand, saves the current one for other projects, or deletes saved ones.
App Store App
Link the project to your app on the App Store. See Linking your App Store app.
Headline
Font, weight, color, tracking, leading, case (As Typed or All Caps) and Warp (Bulge, Pinch, Arch or Wave, with an amount). Smaller / Larger resize headlines.
Supporting Text
Font, weight, color and leading for the smaller line under the headline, plus Smaller / Larger.
Layout
Text alignment, the margin around each scene, and Tone (Light or Dark).
Background
Solid, Linear, Radial or Image, with presets. Continue across all scenes makes one background flow behind every scene, like a panorama.
Devices
The Brand device: Frame (Official or Mock), Device and Color, plus Pose and Shadow for mock frames (As Placed leaves each device as it is). Every device on screens that follow the Brand uses it. Device Library downloads more Apple devices.
Logo
Drop your app icon or logo onto the empty slot, or click it to choose a file. Then Place on Scene, Replace… or Remove.
Apply Everywhere
Follow Brand on All Scenes turns Follow Brand back on for every scene you styled by hand.
Changes apply straight awayThere's nothing to refresh. A short note at the bottom of the Stage tells you how many scenes updated, and which items were left alone.

How the Brand works

Each scene has a Follow Brand switch in its Scene Settings. While it's on, that scene's background, headline and subheadline style, and devices all come from the Brand, and those controls are locked in the Inspector with a Set by Brand note.

Follow Brand on
The scene updates whenever the Brand changes.
Follow Brand off
The scene keeps its current look, and everything on it can be styled by hand. Brand changes leave it alone.
Detach one item
Right-click a headline, subheadline or device on the Stage and choose Detach from Brand, or click Detach in its Set by Brand note in the Inspector. Only that item stops following; the rest of the scene still does. In Layers it shows a broken-link icon and Detached.
Reattach
Right-click it again and choose Reattach to Brand. It picks up the current Brand style.
Link to Brand
Any text you added yourself can join the Brand too: right-click it and choose Link to Brand. It becomes a headline or supporting text and takes the Brand's style.
Templates and your Brand
If you've customized the Brand, choosing a new template asks whether to Keep My Brand (only the layout changes) or Use Template Look. Scenes you styled by hand and detached items are kept either way.

Linking your App Store app

If your app is already on the App Store, link it and the project takes on that app's details. In Backstage › Brand › App Store App, click Link App Store App, then paste your app's App Store link or search for it by name.

Before anything changes, PreviewStage asks. When you click Link App, in one step:

  • The project takes your app's name.
  • Your app's icon is saved, and becomes the Brand logo if you haven't set one.
  • Your live App Store screenshots are downloaded at full size and added to Assets, ready to drop into any device. Your scenes stay as they are.
  • Preview Mode shows your real ratings, price, age rating, category, developer, languages, size and description, and lets you compare your design with what's live.

One ⌘Z undoes it all. Your Header and Search Results images are never touched.

Refresh from the App Store
Reads your listing again: new ratings, version, description or name. Your scenes are left alone.
View on the App Store
Opens your app's page in your browser.
Link a Different App…
Links another app, with the same confirmation.
Unlink
Goes back to placeholder details. Your scenes stay as they are.
Only your public listing is readLinking reads the same public information anyone sees on the App Store, using Apple's lookup. There's no account to sign in to, and nothing is uploaded. It works for apps that are already live.
Chapter 5

Stage

The Stage is the center of the window: your scenes at their real proportions. Click to select, drag to move, and drag the handles to resize or rotate. Double-click text to edit it.

Moving around
Scroll with two fingers to pan; pinch or ⌘-scroll to zoom. Double-tap with two fingers to switch between fitting everything and 100%.
Smart guides
While you drag, a dashed red line marks the center of the scene and turns solid green when your layer is exactly centered. Layers snap to the center, edges and other layers. Turn guides on or off with the ruler button or ⌘;.
Stage controls
At the bottom: zoom out, zoom level, zoom in, Zoom to Fit, Guides, and Panorama View (scenes joined edge to edge, for designs that flow across them).
Adding screenshots
Drop images straight onto a device. An empty device shows Drop a screenshot with a Choose… button.
Apple's guidelines card
If a device with an official Apple bezel is rotated, tilted, cropped, shadowed or covered, a card appears in the top-right corner of the Stage explaining what Apple asks for. Click Straighten to fix it, or Use Mock to switch to a mock frame. Hidden devices don't count.
Right-click
Cut, copy, duplicate, arrange and span layers. Devices also offer Add, Replace or Remove Screenshot. Items that follow the Brand offer Detach from Brand. With several layers selected, the menu acts on all of them, including detaching or reattaching them in one step.
Copy and paste style
Copy Style (⌥⌘C) and Paste Style (⌥⌘V) copy only the look (font, colors, shadow and so on), never the words or position.
Brand updates
After you change the Brand, a short note at the bottom of the Stage shows what updated, then slides away.
Orientation changes
If you switch the project between portrait and landscape, your layout is kept and devices turn with it. A bar offers to re-apply your template, laid out fresh for the new shape.
Chapter 6

Capture from Simulator

Take screenshots of your app straight from Xcode's Simulator, into the right device, without saving files. Click Capture in the toolbar (⌃⌘C).

  1. Run your app from Xcode (⌘R) and go to the screen you want.
  2. In PreviewStage, click Capture next to the Simulator. The screenshot goes into the device on the scene shown under Into.
  3. Go to your app's next screen and capture again. With Move to the next scene after each capture on, each one fills the next scene.
Clean status bar
Shows 9:41, full battery and full signal while capturing, like Apple's own screenshots. The Simulator goes back to normal afterwards.
The right device
Each Simulator is matched to its device (iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch, Vision Pro or Apple TV), and the scene's device changes to match when it doesn't.
Mac apps
In Mac projects, Capture lists your Mac's open windows instead. macOS asks once for Screen Recording permission, and only the window you choose is captured.
Chapter 7

Inspector

The panel on the right always shows settings for what you're working on. Long sections can be collapsed with the chevron next to their title.

When nothing is selected: scene & project

Scene card
Which scene you're on (for example Scene 1 of 3), with arrows to move to the previous or next scene.
Scene Settings
The scene's name and the Follow Brand switch (see How the Brand works). With it off, the scene's own background controls appear. Duplicate and Delete act on the whole scene, and Apply Layout to All Scenes arranges every scene like this one.
Project
Project name, platform, devices, size and orientation. Social media projects also show their Network, which you can change: your scenes keep their shape when the new network has it.
All Scenes
In All Scenes view, with nothing picked, the Inspector shows settings for every scene, and Edit Brand takes you to the Brand. Click a scene to edit just that one.

When a layer is selected

A path at the top, like ‹ Scene 1 / Device Layer, shows where the layer lives. Click ‹ Scene 1 to go back to the scene's settings. Next to the layer's name are buttons to lock and hide it.

Arrange
Position (X, Y), size (W, H) and rotation, with buttons to rotate 90° left or right. The align bar lines the layer up with the scene. Order moves it forward or backward. Span Scenes lets it stretch across neighboring scenes.
Device
Frame (Official or Mock), Device, Color, and for iPhone Duo, Fold. Then the Screen it shows, with Replace… and Remove, Fit (Fill or Fit) and Orientation. While the device follows the Brand, frame, device and color are set by the Brand.
Presentation
Poses, each previewed with your device: Front, Turn Left, Turn Right, Lay Back, Float Left, Float Right, Lean Left, Lean Right, Close Up and Hero. Fine-tune with Turn, Tilt, Spin and Depth (from Subtle to Close-up, as if the camera is right by the device), and pick a Shadow: Floating, Soft or None. Poses are made for mock frames: Apple asks that its own bezels stay upright, and the Stage reminds you if one doesn't.
Appearance
Opacity, blur, corner radius and shadow (color, radius and offset).
Delete Layer
At the bottom of every layer's settings.

Text, images, shapes and symbols

Text
Font, weight, color, tracking, alignment and caps, and Warp to bend it: Bulge, Pinch, Arch or Wave, with an amount. Edit the words themselves by double-clicking the text on the Stage. Translate, in the corner of the text box (or right-click the text on the Stage), translates it into another language with Apple's translation, on your Mac, keeping the style. While the text follows the Brand, its style is locked (Set by Brand · Detach · Scene Settings); size and the words stay editable.
Image & Crop
The image source, Fit and Mask, then crop with Scale and Offset. Reset Crop starts over. Remove Background (also in the image's right-click menu) lifts out the subject with Apple's subject lifting, on your Mac, and leaves the rest transparent; the original stays in Assets.
Shape
Shape type (including Triangle and Wave), fill (Solid, Linear, Radial or Image) and stroke.
Symbol
Click the symbol to open the browser (below), plus twelve Quick picks, Color and Weight.

When several layers are selected

Hold ⇧ or ⌘ and click to select more than one layer. The Inspector then offers alignment, distribution (horizontally or vertically), Group and Ungroup, plus opacity and shadow for all of them.

Chapter 8

Header & Search Results

On iOS 27 and iPadOS 27, the App Store adds two new images, which Apple calls creative assets. PreviewStage makes them in the same project as your screenshots, with the same Brand.

Header
A wide picture across the top of your product page, the first thing people see when they open your app. Apple's advice: express your brand, with one clear idea. 3840 × 1646 (21:9).
Search Results
Shown under your app in App Store search, in place of your screenshots. Apple's advice: make what your app does obvious at a glance, and show the real experience. 3840 × 2560 (3:2).
Universal
One picture Apple crops for both places. Turn on Use one image for both in Backstage. 5244 × 2950 (16:9), PNG.

Both are optional: without a Header, your page starts with your icon and name, and without a Search Results image, search shows your first screenshots. Creative assets are available for iPhone and iPad projects.

Making one

  1. In Backstage › Scenes, find Creative Assets under your scenes.
  2. Click Header or Search Results. It opens on the Stage at full size, starting with your app's name in your Brand.
  3. Design it like a scene, or pick a template. Text, images, devices, shapes, symbols and Image Playground pictures all work here.
  4. Click a scene in the list to go back to your screenshots.

The Inspector shows the asset's size, a Follow Brand switch, Choose a Template, See It in the App Store (Preview Mode on the right page), Remove, and a short guide to what the asset is for.

What each device shows

You make one image, and Apple crops it differently on each device. The Stage outlines each one:

iPad
Shows the whole Header.
iPhone
Shows the middle of the Header, with the status bar and the back and share buttons over the top.
iPhone & iPad
Search Results show the whole image.
Art Safe Area
The green box, from Apple's own templates: the part every device shows. Keep your name and key art inside it. Devices and scenery can run past it, and off the bottom edge, as in Apple's examples.
A note on the cropsThe green Art Safe Area is Apple's exact measurement. Apple doesn't publish the iPhone and iPad crops, so those outlines are measured from the iOS 27 App Store.

Templates

With a Header, Search Results or Universal image open, Backstage › Templates shows templates made for it, drawn in your Brand's colors with your app's name, tagline and screenshots.

Studio
Headers: Glow Mark, Monument, Pattern Field, Horizon Type, Constellation, Floating Objects, Season Frame and Split Tone. Search Results: Claim + Checklist, Triptych, Before → After, Card Stack, Feature Row, Zoomed Moment and Full Bleed.
Nature
Hills, Forest, Mountains, Ocean, Clouds, Flight, Sunset, Night Sky, Dunes, Sailing and City.
Abstract
Windows, Spotlight, Horizon, Orbits, Ribbons, Glow, Dots, Waves and Rays.
Your App
Logo Lockup, Statement, Lineup, Your Scenes (pictures of your finished scenes), Fan and Simple.

For a Header, each scene shows your name as a wordmark, as in Apple's examples. For Search Results, it shows your promise and your app over the same scene. Every tree, cloud and mountain is an ordinary shape you can move, recolor or delete.

Preview Mode shows the Header at the top of your product page and the Search Results image in search (see Preview Mode). Export saves them full size in a Creative Assets folder, ready to upload in App Store Connect with a new version or in your Asset Library.

Chapter 9

Trailers

Trailers are short videos of your app: App Store videos (Apple calls them App Previews) that autoplay on your product page and in search, or videos for your social network. Open the Trailers tab in Backstage.

Recording takes

A trailer is made of takes: recordings of you using your app.

  1. Run your app in the Simulator from Xcode. It appears under Record in Backstage. If nothing is running, Backstage tells you what to do.
  2. Click Record. The button shows Starting… for a moment, then a red dot and the seconds counting.
  3. Use your app in the Simulator, then click Stop. The take is saved (a card in the middle of the Stage shows its progress) and added to the end of the trailer as Take 1, Take 2 and so on.

Record as long as you like. You can also click Import Takes… or drop screen recordings onto the Stage; imported takes keep their file names. Clean status bar shows 9:41 and a full battery while recording.

Editing

Timeline
Takes in order with their frames, and captions under them. Click or drag anywhere to move the playhead; press Space or click the picture to play and pause.
Takes
Click a take to select it, then drag its ends to trim it. Right-click to move it earlier or later, or delete it. The Inspector shows the take's trim and order.
Captions
Click Caption to add one at the playhead, in your Brand's headline font. Drag it to move it, drag its end to change how long it shows, and set its words and position (top or bottom) in the Inspector.
Trailer settings
Name, size, orientation and Fade between takes. Takes recorded the other way round get black bars instead of being cropped.
Poster
App Store only: the frame shown when autoplay is off. Move the playhead and click Use Playhead.
Soundtrack
Add Soundtrack plays music under the trailer, with a volume slider; it fades out at the end. Use music you have the rights to.
Header card
At the top of the Inspector: the trailer, take or caption you're editing, with arrows to step to the next one.

Length and the red line

App Store videos are 15 to 30 seconds. A red App Store ends line marks 30 seconds on the timeline: everything before it is exported and uploaded, and anything after it stays in the editor but isn't. The badge next to the time tells you when a trailer is too short or Exports the first 30 s.

Export Trailer… in the Inspector saves an .mp4 at Apple's exact size: H.264 at 30 frames per second with AAC stereo sound (a silent track is added when there's no music, as App Store Connect expects). Uploading sends it with your screenshots: see Uploading to App Store Connect.

Apple's rulesApp Store videos show only footage from your app: no hands, people or devices. Captions should stay on long enough to read, and prices and dates don't belong in them. Backstage lists these rules under the Trailers.

Videos for your Header and Search Results

On iPhone and iPad, a trailer can also be the video for a creative asset: set Use For in the Inspector to Header, Search Results or Universal. It takes that asset's size (3840 × 1646, 3840 × 2560 or 5244 × 2950), any length, and a poster frame, and exports as an HEVC .mp4. Brand and mood footage are fine here, as long as they relate to your app; Apple suggests making it loop smoothly and work without sound. Upload these in App Store Connect › Asset Library; they aren't sent with your App Previews.

Social media videos

In a social media project, trailers follow your network instead of Apple's rules. Each trailer picks a Format in the Inspector, with its size and length limit, for example:

Instagram
Reel and Feed Video up to 3 minutes; Story up to 60 seconds.
TikTok
Vertical video up to 10 minutes.
YouTube
Short up to 3 minutes; regular Video with no limit.
Facebook
Reel and Feed Video; Story up to 60 seconds.
X
Landscape, Vertical or Square, up to 2 minutes 20 seconds.
LinkedIn
Square, Vertical or Landscape, up to 10 minutes.
Threads
Vertical or Portrait, up to 5 minutes.
Pinterest
Video Pins from 4 seconds to 15 minutes.

The red line sits at the format's limit, labeled with the format, like Story limit; formats without a limit have none. Backstage shows tips for your network, any running Simulator can be recorded, and a project can hold up to ten trailers. Networks change their limits from time to time.

Chapter 10

Image Playground

Choose Image ▸ Create with Image Playground… to make a picture with Apple Intelligence. Describe what you want, pick a style, and click Done: the picture is added to the project's images and placed on the current scene or asset.

  • Select a text layer first and its words become the starting idea. Select an image first and it becomes the starting picture.
  • Apple's on-device styles are offered: Animation, Illustration and Sketch.
  • Image Playground needs Apple Intelligence. If it's off on your Mac, the menu says so.

ChatGPT styles

If you use ChatGPT with Apple Intelligence, turn on Include ChatGPT styles in PreviewStage ▸ Settings… (⌘,), or choose Image Playground Settings… in the Image menu. It's off by default: ChatGPT styles send your description, and any starting image, to OpenAI, and they need ChatGPT turned on in System Settings › Apple Intelligence & Siri.

Chapter 11

Device frames

Every device can use one of two kinds of frame. Switch with Frame in the Inspector, for one device or for all scenes at once.

Mock

PreviewStage's own drawn frames. Always available, in several finishes, and free to tilt, rotate or shadow however you like.

Official

Apple's own product bezels, downloaded from Apple when you choose them. They match the real devices exactly, and Apple asks that they're used as-is.

Only downloaded Apple devices can be chosen. Ones you haven't downloaded are dimmed, with a ↓ button to download them right there. If you switch to Official before downloading anything, PreviewStage keeps your mock and offers the download. Your device switches over when it's ready.

Mock and Official each remember their own choice, so switching back and forth never loses your setup. To use the same device everywhere, set it once in Backstage › Brand › Devices.

Chapter 12

Device Library

Open it from the round button on Home, from Download More… in the Device menu, or with ⌥⌘L.

  • Filter by All, iPhone, iPad, Mac, Watch or TV, and turn on Installed to see only what you've downloaded.
  • Each pack shows Apple's own preview of its devices. Click Get to download it; installed packs show the real bezel and a green check.
  • The first time, you'll be asked to accept Apple's license for its marketing artwork. The bezels are Apple's, and they're always free in PreviewStage.
Chapter 13

Preview Mode

Click ▶ in the toolbar (⌥⌘P) to see your screenshots in the App Store on the device they're for: the iPhone App Store in an iPhone, the iPad App Store in an iPad, a Mac App Store window, or the Apple TV store on a TV. Apple Watch screenshots appear in the iPhone App Store, as they do for customers.

Product Page
Your Header (if you've made one), your icon, name and Get button, then your screenshots. Swipe them, use ← →, or click one to see it full size. Scroll down for the rest of the page: What's New, Ratings & Reviews with real recent reviews, More by the developer, and Information (provider, size, category, compatibility, languages, age rating and price). With a linked app these are all its real details.
Search Result
Your app as it appears in App Store search, with your Search Results image or your first screenshots. iPhone and iPad only.
Focus
One screenshot at a time, full size. Use the arrow buttons, ← → or Space.
Your Design / Live Now
With a linked app, this switch at the top right shows the screenshots on the App Store today on the same page, so you can compare them with your new design.

A brand-new app has no ratings yet, so none are shown; a linked app shows its real ratings and details. Scenes with an empty device are left out, and a note tells you which ones. Press Esc or close the window to return to the editor.

Chapter 14

Exporting

Click Export (⌘E).

  1. Choose which scenes to export: All, Current, or Choose to pick them.
  2. Under Sizes, tick the sizes you need. Sizes Apple requires are marked Required. Other sizes are adapted automatically from your layout.
  3. Under File Names, pick how files are named. Each choice shows the name your first file will get: 01 Scene 1.png, 01.png or Daybook 01.png.
  4. Under Creative Assets, choose whether to include your Header and Search Results images.
  5. Export. Images are saved as PNG without transparency, which App Store Connect requires.

With Organize into folders by size on, each size gets its own folder, like iPhone 6.9-inch Display 1320x2868, and creative assets go in a Creative Assets folder.

Before you exportEvery device needs a screenshot: blank scenes can't ship, so export waits until they're filled. If an Apple bezel doesn't follow Apple's guidelines, you'll see a warning first.
Chapter 15

Uploading to App Store Connect

Send your screenshots, and your trailers, straight to your app's listing in App Store Connect, without saving files. Choose File › Upload to App Store Connect… (⇧⌘U), or click Upload… in the Export sheet.

Connect once

  1. In App Store Connect, open Users and Access › Integrations › App Store Connect API.
  2. Make a key with the App Manager role and download its .p8 file (Apple lets you download it once).
  3. In PreviewStage › Settings › App Store Connect, add the Issuer ID and the .p8 file. The Key ID fills itself in. The status turns green when it's connected.

One key works for every app in your account. The private key is kept in your Mac's Keychain, readable only while your Mac is unlocked, and never leaves your Mac except to sign requests to Apple.

Upload

  1. Pick the app, the version you're preparing, and the language.
  2. Tick the sizes to send. Each one replaces the screenshots already there for that size and language.
  3. With trailers in the project, Include trailers sends them too (trailers shorter than 15 seconds are left out; longer ones are cut at 30), with your poster frame.
  4. Click Upload. When everything's sent you'll see Curtain call.
Only to AppleUploads go only to Apple's servers. Uploading is for App Store projects; social media projects export files for you to post.

Chapter 17

Help & FAQ

An Apple device in the menu is dimmed. Why can't I choose it?

It hasn't been downloaded yet. Click its ↓ button, or open the Device Library and click Get. When the download finishes, you can choose it.

Why won't my project export?

At least one device has no screenshot. Blank scenes can't go on the App Store, so add a screenshot to every device, or delete the empty scene. The export sheet shows how many are missing.

What does “Apple's guidelines” mean?

Apple asks that its product bezels are shown upright, uncropped, without added shadows or reflections, and with nothing on top. If you want a tilted or shadowed look, switch that device to a Mock frame, which you can style freely.

Why are some scenes missing in Preview Mode?

Scenes with an empty device are left out of the preview. Add a screenshot and they'll appear.

Can I use any SF Symbol?

Thousands of them. Apple's logo and symbols that represent Apple's own products and features aren't available, because Apple's license doesn't allow them in your artwork.

I changed the orientation and my layout looks different.

Your layout is kept and resized for the new shape, and devices turn with it. For a fresh layout, click Re-apply in the bar at the bottom of the Stage, or press ⌘Z to undo the change.

Where did Backstage go?

It may be hidden. Click the Backstage button at the left of the toolbar, or press ⌃⌘S.

Why can't I change a headline's font or a device?

It follows the Brand, so its style is set in Backstage › Brand. Change it there to update every scene, turn off Follow Brand in Scene Settings to style that scene by hand, or right-click the item and choose Detach from Brand.

What does the broken-link icon in Layers mean?

That item was detached from the Brand, so it keeps its own style while the rest of its scene follows the Brand. Right-click it on the Stage and choose Reattach to Brand to link it again.

Do I need a Header or Search Results image?

No, both are optional. Without them, the App Store shows your icon and name at the top of your page, and your first screenshots in search. They only appear on iOS 27 and iPadOS 27.

Why is the Art Safe Area so small?

It's the part of your image that every device shows. Outside it, some devices crop. Keep your name and key art inside the green box, and let backgrounds, scenery and devices fill the rest.

Why don't I see Creative Assets in my project?

They're for iPhone and iPad App Store projects. Mac, Apple Watch, Apple TV, Vision Pro and social media projects don't have them, because the App Store doesn't use them there.

Image Playground is greyed out.

It needs Apple Intelligence. Turn it on in System Settings › Apple Intelligence & Siri, on a Mac that supports it.

Where are the ChatGPT styles in Image Playground?

They're off by default because they send your description to OpenAI. Turn on Include ChatGPT styles in PreviewStage ▸ Settings, after setting up ChatGPT in System Settings › Apple Intelligence & Siri.

Where are my live screenshots after linking?

In Backstage › Assets, named after your app (for example My App Live 01, My App Live 02). Linking never changes your scenes: drag a screenshot onto a device, or right-click it and choose Use in Selected Device. Press ⌘Z once to undo the whole link.

Why can't I find my app when linking?

Linking works for apps that are live on the App Store. Search looks in your Mac's App Store country and for your project's kind of device; pasting your app's App Store link always finds it.

Why doesn't a linked app show my subtitle?

Apple's public listing doesn't include an app's subtitle, so PreviewStage uses your first scene's headline in its place.

Record shows no Simulator. What do I do?

Run your app from Xcode (⌘R) on a Simulator. For an App Store project it needs to be the project's kind of device, for example an iPhone Simulator for an iPhone project. It appears in Backstage › Trailers, and in Capture, within a couple of seconds.

Why can't I export my trailer?

App Store videos must be at least 15 seconds long; social formats have their own minimum. Add or lengthen takes until the badge next to the time says Length OK.

My trailer is longer than 30 seconds. What happens?

Everything before the red App Store ends line is exported and uploaded; the rest stays in the editor. Trim or reorder takes to choose what's in the first 30 seconds.

Is my App Store Connect key safe?

The private key is stored in your Mac's Keychain, only readable while your Mac is unlocked, and it never leaves your Mac. PreviewStage only connects to Apple with it.

Where are my projects saved?

On your Mac, automatically, as you work. Open them any time from Home (⇧⌘0).
