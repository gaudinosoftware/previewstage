Privacy

Your work stays on your Mac.

PreviewStage doesn't collect, share or sell personal information. Your projects stay on your Mac.

Your projects

Projects, screenshots, trailers and settings are stored on your Mac. PreviewStage has no account, and it doesn't upload your projects or images anywhere, unless you choose to upload to App Store Connect (below).

Uploading to App Store Connect

If you add an App Store Connect API key, PreviewStage uses it to send the screenshots and trailers you choose to your own app in App Store Connect. The private key is stored in your Mac's Keychain, readable only while your Mac is unlocked, and never leaves your Mac: it's used only to sign requests. The Issuer ID and Key ID are kept in PreviewStage's settings on your Mac. PreviewStage connects only to Apple's servers, and only to check the connection (when you open that part of Settings or test it) or to upload. You can remove the key at any time in Settings.

Capturing from the Simulator and your Mac

Screenshots and recordings of your app are made on your Mac with Xcode's Simulator, and saved in your project. While capturing, PreviewStage can set the Simulator's status bar to 9:41 with a full battery, and puts it back afterwards. Temporary files are deleted once they're in your project. For Mac apps, macOS asks for Screen Recording permission, and only the window you choose is captured. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Translation and text recognition

Translating text uses Apple's Translation on your Mac; a language may need a one-time download from Apple the first time you use it. Reading the text in a screenshot uses Apple's Vision on your Mac. Your words and images aren't sent to PreviewStage or anyone else.

Soundtracks and imported files

Music, recordings and images you add are copied into your project on your Mac. PreviewStage doesn't post anything to social networks: you export files and post them yourself.

Downloading Apple's device bezels

When you choose to download official device bezels, PreviewStage downloads them directly from Apple's website. Like any download, this request goes to Apple's servers, which see your IP address under Apple's own privacy policy. Nothing about you or your projects is sent.

Linking your App Store app

When you link a project to an app, PreviewStage reads that app's public App Store listing from Apple's lookup service: its name, icon, screenshots, ratings, details, recent public reviews and the developer's other apps, the same information anyone sees on the App Store. Preview Mode loads those apps' icons from Apple. The search words or link you enter are sent to Apple to find the app. There's no account, nothing is uploaded, and what's downloaded is saved in your project on your Mac.

Device Library previews

The Device Library shows Apple's own preview pictures of each device pack, loaded from Apple's website. As with the downloads, Apple's servers see your IP address; nothing about you or your projects is sent.

Image Playground

Image Playground is Apple's. PreviewStage opens it, and receives only the finished picture you choose, which is saved in your project on your Mac. By default it uses Apple's on-device styles. If you turn on Include ChatGPT styles in Settings (it's off by default), your description, and any starting image you choose, can be sent to OpenAI through Apple Intelligence when you use those styles, under Apple's and OpenAI's terms. PreviewStage itself sends nothing.

Subscriptions

Designing and previewing are free; exporting needs a subscription. Purchases are made through Apple, and PreviewStage never sees your payment details. To check whether you're subscribed, PreviewStage uses RevenueCat, a subscription service. RevenueCat receives an anonymous ID created for this app, your purchase records from Apple, and basic device information such as your macOS version, country and app version. It never receives your name, your email address, your projects or your images. RevenueCat handles this under its own privacy policy. You can manage or cancel a subscription in your Apple Account's subscription settings.

Analytics and tracking

PreviewStage contains no analytics, advertising or tracking. Subscription records are used only to unlock exporting.
