Privacy

Your work stays on your Mac.

PreviewStage doesn't collect, share or sell any personal information.

Your projects

Projects, screenshots and settings are stored on your Mac. PreviewStage has no account, and it doesn't upload your projects or images anywhere.

Downloading Apple's device bezels

When you choose to download official device bezels, PreviewStage downloads them directly from Apple's website. Like any download, this request goes to Apple's servers, which see your IP address under Apple's own privacy policy. Nothing about you or your projects is sent.

Analytics and tracking

PreviewStage contains no analytics, advertising or tracking.

Links

Links to Apple's design resources and guidelines open in your web browser.
